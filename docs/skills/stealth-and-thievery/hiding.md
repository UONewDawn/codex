# Hiding

## Overview

Hiding allows you to become invisible to players and monsters.

Often paired with Stealth for movement.

Starting items if you choose this skill in character creation: Cloak.

## Effects

Crossing paths with someone hidden will give a message "Being perfectly rested, you shove something invisible out of the way."

Speaking or taking offensive actions will reveal you.

Looting your own corpse will not reveal you, but looting others will.

You can't hide while in combat. This means anyone fighting you who can see you and is close enough. How close depends on your Hiding: 18 tiles at low skill, shrinking to 8 tiles at GM. An enemy further away or out of sight doesn't stop you.

Hiding inside a house where you're at least a friend always works, whatever your skill. Standing right next to a house also makes it much easier.

Using most skills will reveal you, except for Detecting Hidden, Item Identification, Anatomy, Arms Lore, Animal Lore, Evaluate Intelligence, Forensic Evaluation, and Poisoning.

You can be revealed by the skill Detecting Hidden or by the spell Reveal.

## Egg bombs

Egg bombs can be used to hide in combat and have a 25 second cooldown.

They are made with Cooking at 90+ skill.

## At GM

At GM, Hiding never fails as long as you're not in combat (see above). The only thing that stops you is someone fighting you within 8 tiles who can see you.

GM Hiding also lets you hide and stealth in one go: with Stealth above 80, using Stealth while visible hides you first. See [Stealth](stealth.md#at-gm).

### Monsters that detect hidden

Some monsters have Detecting Hidden and check every so often for hidden players they can see. Your protection against them comes from the **lower** of your Hiding and Stealth, so Hiding alone doesn't help. You need Stealth as well.

| Monster's Detecting Hidden | GM Hiding, no Stealth | GM Hiding + GM Stealth |
|:--------------------------:|:---------------------:|:----------------------:|
| 50                         | 42%                   | 7%                     |
| 80                         | 67%                   | 32%                    |
| 100                        | 83%                   | 49%                    |

The chance is per check, and a monster with Detecting Hidden always has at least a tenth of its skill as its chance.

With GM Hiding, any Stealth of about 62 or more gives the full protection. Past that, Hiding is what limits it.

## Stealth

Stealth allows you to move while hidden. You need at least 80 Hiding to use it.

## Tracking

Players that have both Tracking and Detecting Hidden have a chance at tracking hidden players.

At GM Tracking, they can track up to 100 tiles away, 50 tiles within a dungeon.

Both players must be in the same region and dungeon level.

The chance is calculated by using the total Tracking and Detecting Hidden against the total Hiding and Stealth of the other player.

After the base chance is calculated, is then also reduced based on the distance between the players.

## Training

Train from Thief NPCs to reach around 50.

Repeatedly Hide until reaching 100.

While training this skill you can also gain Dexterity (primary) and Intelligence (secondary). Visit the [stats](../../game-mechanics/character/stats.md) page to learn more.
