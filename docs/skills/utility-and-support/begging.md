# Begging

## Overview

Begging allows you to ask NPCs for gold. While not a combat or crafting skill, it can provide a small source of income and is useful for roleplay.

Begging negatively affects your karma.

Starting items if you choose this skill in character creation: Gnarled Staff.

## Trick or Treat

During the Halloween rift season, players with 100 Begging have a small chance of receiving a rare Halloween treat instead of candy when they trick or treat. For more information visit the [Rift Seasons](../../custom-systems/rift-seasons.md#trick-or-treat) page.

## Training

Train from Begging child NPCs (3714, 2509) to reach around 50.

Repeatedly beg NPCs until reaching 100.

While training this skill you can also gain Dexterity (primary) and Intelligence (secondary). Visit the [stats](../../game-mechanics/character/stats.md) page to learn more.
