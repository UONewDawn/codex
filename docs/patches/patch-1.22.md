# SERVER PATCH 1.22

## November Xth, 2026

This patch...

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Enhancements

- The Auction House now asks before it spends your gold. Buyout, placing a bid and cancelling your own listing each open a confirmation showing the item and the exact amount, so a misclick or an extra zero no longer costs you
- Creating an auction now always opens a review screen with every setting before the listing goes live. The review no longer drops your title, category, tags or auto-extend, and it shows the real fee: a cut of the final sale price, not a listing fee
- Changing the category or auto-extend while creating an auction no longer clears the title you typed
- Spirit Summoners now need at least 80.1 Magery for Spirit Speak's 8th circle summoning bonus. Before, a character with GM Spirit Speak and no Magery could summon daemons and elementals 80% of the time. Energy Vortex no longer gets the bonus
- The Constellation Ledger has been redesigned. You can filter by night period or your own discoveries, search by name or astronomer, sort any column, and open a constellation to see its stars. See [Astronomy](../custom-systems/astronomy.md#the-constellation-ledger)
- The Interior Decorator has a new panel. Besides rotating and raising or lowering, it can now turn items both ways, slide them one tile north, east, south or west, lock down, release and secure, undo your last arrangement, and bring the last item you moved to your feet. Your old decorator upgrades the first time you double-click it in your backpack. See [Housing](../game-mechanics/housing.md#interior-decorator)
- You can now secure a container with your house's last free lockdown. Before, securing needed two free lockdowns while locking down an item needed only one
- Order vs. Chaos: friendly fire no longer costs you your gear. When you die in an outpost, you keep your belongings if the enemy did at least half the damage, and damage from yourself or your own faction, like a friend's Earthquake, is now left out of that count. A death dealt entirely by your own side is still a normal death and your corpse can be looted. See [Order vs. Chaos](../custom-systems/order-vs-chaos.md#dying-in-an-outpost)
- Plant care messages such as watering, pouring potions, planting seeds, gathering, pollinating and emptying a bowl now appear as system messages in your journal instead of over the plant
- The plant gump has a compact view. Click **Compact** in its top-right corner for a small card that still shows your plant's health, water and ailments, tells you in one line what it needs, and has every water, potion and harvest button. Click **Expand** to go back. Every plant opens the way you last left it. See [Gardening](../game-mechanics/gardening.md#compact-view)
- A bowl of dirt no longer dries out before you plant a seed in it. Water a batch of bowls first and plant at your own pace. Soggy dirt still drains back to Just right
- Beehives no longer overcount sweetened water. The hive gump could show more sweetened sources than water sources, and one sugared barrel gave the full +2 water bonus instead of +1
- The Halloween lanterns are now blessed: the jack-o-lantern and grinning jack-o-lantern from the Reaper's Hollow Market, and the Lantern of Souls and Spectral Lantern from the Phantom Fleet Exchange. Ones you already own were blessed too

## 🪙 Coin Collecting

- Rarer pieces now turn up much more often from treasure chests: Very Rare about four times as often, Rare twice as often and Scarce a third more often. Common pieces turn up about a third less often, and Uncommon is unchanged. Duplicates are worth trading: other collectors are hunting what you already have
- The Master Set is now the Base Set plus one example of every kind of variety. Your book shows how many kinds you've found
- New achievements for mounting 10, 25, 50 and 75 issues, and 5, 10 and 25 varieties. See [Coin Collecting](../custom-systems/coin-collecting.md#achievements)

## ⚔️ Dueling

- New **Custom** duels: when you post a duel on the board, pick Custom and choose your rules: InstaHit (swapping weapons doesn't reset your swing), no potions, no bandages and no poisoning (the Poisoning skill and poisoned weapons; the Poison and Poison Field spells still work). Mix any of them
- The rules show on the challenge before anyone accepts and are announced at the start of every round. Custom duels count toward your record and achievements like casual duels but never touch your rating. See [Dueling](../custom-systems/dueling.md#custom-rules)

## 🛡️ Order vs. Chaos: Faction Helms & Rank 6

- New faction helms at Rank 1: the **Serpent Silver Close Helm** for the Order and the **Chaos Crimson Bone Helm** for Chaos, 300 War Tokens. They're blessed, can't be dyed and never wear out. A faction helm adds about **9 AR** to your status window, the same as an invulnerability plate helm, but with no meditation or stamina penalty, so mages and dexers can both wear their colours into a fight. They only work while your guild belongs to that faction. The mask dyes are still on sale
- Leaving your faction now takes your regalia off: faction helms, cloaks and robes go to your backpack, or your bank if the pack is full
- New Rank 6. Rank 5 is now **Marshal of Order** / **Scourge of Chaos** at 2,500 Faction Points and unlocks the helm in Protector Gold or Dread Ember. **Lord Protector** / **Dread Lord** and the elite cloak move to Rank 6 at 4,500
- The Order and Chaos War Horses can only be ridden by Rank 4 and higher members of their faction
- The faction stores no longer sell to players on side-switch probation. See [Order vs. Chaos](../custom-systems/order-vs-chaos.md#faction-stores)

## 🗄️ Storage Rack

- Racks now hold **every Magery scroll**, first to eighth circle, plus **Poison Potions**, **Kindling**, **Wool**, **Bone**, empty **Bags** and **Trapped Pouches**
- Trapped Pouches are pouches with Magic Trap on them. They come back out ready to use with **[Pouch**
- Bags and trapped pouches have to be empty. Drop one on the rack to store it. Sweeping your backpack takes them too, but never takes the bag you set for withdrawals
- New jump buttons go straight to a section: **Stock**, **Crafting**, or a scroll circle from **1** to **8**. They're at the bottom of the rack and at the top of the loadout editor
- Item pictures are now centred in their rows, so tall ones like the harp no longer cover the names. See [Storage Rack](../custom-systems/storage-rack.md#what-it-holds)
