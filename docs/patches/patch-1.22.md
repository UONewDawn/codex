# SERVER PATCH 1.22

## November Xth, 2026

This patch...

## ⚔️ Dueling

- New **Custom** duels: when you post a duel on the board, pick Custom and choose your rules: InstaHit (swapping weapons doesn't reset your swing), no potions, no bandages and no poisoning. Mix any of them
- The rules show on the challenge before anyone accepts and are announced at the start of every round. Custom duels count toward your record and achievements like casual duels but never touch your rating. See [Dueling](../custom-systems/dueling.md#custom-rules)

## 🛡️ Order vs. Chaos: Faction Helms & Rank 6

- New faction helms at Rank 1: the **Serpent Silver Close Helm** for the Order and the **Chaos Crimson Bone Helm** for Chaos, 300 War Tokens. They're blessed, can't be dyed and never wear out, with an armor rating of 48 (the same as an invulnerability leather cap) and no meditation or stamina penalty, so mages and dexers can both wear their colours into a fight. They only work while your guild belongs to that faction. The mask dyes are still on sale
- Leaving your faction now takes your regalia off: faction helms, cloaks and robes go to your backpack, or your bank if the pack is full
- New Rank 6. Rank 5 is now **Marshal of Order** / **Scourge of Chaos** at 2,500 Faction Points and unlocks the helm in Protector Gold or Dread Ember. **Lord Protector** / **Dread Lord** and the elite cloak move to Rank 6 at 4,500
- The Order and Chaos War Horses can only be ridden by Rank 4 and higher members of their faction
- The faction stores no longer sell to players on side-switch probation. See [Order vs. Chaos](../custom-systems/order-vs-chaos.md#faction-stores)

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Enhancements

- The Auction House now asks before it spends your gold. Buyout, placing a bid and cancelling your own listing each open a confirmation showing the item and the exact amount, so a misclick or an extra zero no longer costs you
- Creating an auction now always opens a review screen with every setting before the listing goes live. The review no longer drops your title, category, tags or auto-extend, and it shows the real fee: a cut of the final sale price, not a listing fee
- Changing the category or auto-extend while creating an auction no longer clears the title you typed
- Spirit Summoners now need at least 80.1 Magery for Spirit Speak's 8th circle summoning bonus. Before, a character with GM Spirit Speak and no Magery could summon daemons and elementals 80% of the time. Energy Vortex no longer gets the bonus, as the Spirit Summoner page always said
