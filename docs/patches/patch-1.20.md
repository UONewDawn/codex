## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Enhancements

- The countdown on an explosion potion will only display for the person who throws it
- When only one faction answered a War Call, the end-of-call message could name the side that never showed up. It now names the side that came
- Food only stacks with food made by the same cook, so shop-bought food can no longer pass as home-cooked for cooking BODs. Splitting a stack keeps its "prepared by"

## 🧺 The Orphanages of Britannia

Orphanages have officially opened in **Britain**, **Moonglow**, **New Minoc**, **Ocllo** and **Paws**. Their children wander the streets nearby, and they will tell you exactly how they're doing. A town that looks after its children will see it in their faces, and they never forget a friend.

See the [Orphanages](../custom-systems/orphanages.md) page for full details.

## 💣 Battle Bombin'

A new arena game for 2 to 8 players. Everyone starts in a corner of a walled maze with a bag of bombs. Blast through the breakable walls, grab the upgrades they leave behind (more bombs, bigger blasts, speed, a detonator), and catch your rivals in the blast. Bombs set each other off, your own blast can catch you, and the last player standing wins. Nobody gets hurt: a blast just knocks you out of the game.

Every game counts toward a monthly and an all-time leaderboard. At the end of each month, the top three go into the Past Seasons list. There are achievements to earn too, with titles for the dedicated: Fuse Lighter, Demolitionist, Powder Keg and Blast Baron. It's all just for fun, with no prizes.

See the [Battle Bombin'](../custom-systems/battle-bombin.md) page for full details.

## Order vs. Chaos: War Call Changes

- If only one faction shows up to a War Call and it holds the outpost when the call ends, it wins **360 season score** (720 at a Shrine of War). Its players are paid as winners: 30 War Tokens, 5 Faction Points and credit toward Hold the Call. It doesn't matter who held the outpost longer, so take it or keep it
- At the War Call outpost, fortification now breaks twice as fast, with a minimum of 3 minutes instead of 6
- Each attacker the owners kill there restores 30% fortification instead of 15%, so defending still pays off
- An even fight on the capture ground still doesn't wear down the fortification

See the [Order vs. Chaos](../custom-systems/order-vs-chaos.md#war-calls) page for full details.
