# SERVER PATCH 1.23

## Release date TBD

## 🎨 Dungeon Dyes

- Double-clicking a Tailor's Dye Kit now opens a kit window: one row per dungeon dye with the material in the kit and a **Craft** button. Pour in every dye material in your backpack with one button, target a single stack, or drop a stack onto the kit
- Shaded Dusty Rose (Terathan Keep) dye can now be crafted
- 24 new blends: carry one bottle each of two dungeon dyes and pour them together on the kit's **Blends** tab to make one bottle of the blend. Each blend is dark-bodied with a glint of the second dye on the folds. Each recipe shows who poured it first on the shard
- After you pour a blend it steeps for 3 days before you can pour another, shared across all of your characters. Crafting regular dungeon dyes is not limited
- New **Crafting / Dungeon Dyes** achievements, with the titles Dungeon Dyer, Colourist of the Depths and Master Dyer. See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#achievements)
- Dye bottles now show their color, and single-clicking one shows the dye's name. Bottles you already have get their color back
- Dye bottles can no longer recolor clothing that a dye tub can't, such as reward robes, shrouds and hoods. Sandals and masks still take dungeon dye
- **This patch updates the client hue file.** Update through the launcher, or blends will look grey

See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#blends).

## 🔭 Astronomy

- Dates in the Constellation Ledger, the constellation view and on star charts are now shown in US Central time
- A used discovery claim now comes back at midnight US Central time, 7 days after you hand it in, instead of at the exact hour you handed it in

See [Astronomy](../custom-systems/astronomy.md#the-rules-of-the-sky).
