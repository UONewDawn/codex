# SERVER PATCH 1.23

## Release date TBD

## 🎨 Dungeon Dyes

- Double-clicking a Tailor's Dye Kit now opens a kit window: one row per dungeon dye with the material in the kit and a **Craft** button. Pour in every dye material in your backpack with one button, target a single stack, or drop a stack onto the kit
- Shaded Dusty Rose (Terathan Keep) dye can now be crafted
- 24 new blends: carry one bottle each of two dungeon dyes and pour them together on the kit's **Blends** tab to make one bottle of the blend. Each blend is dark-bodied with a glint of the second dye on the folds. Each recipe shows who poured it first on the shard
- After you pour a blend it steeps for 3 days before you can pour another, shared by every character on all of your accounts. Crafting regular dungeon dyes is not limited
- New **Crafting / Dungeon Dyes** achievements, with the titles Dungeon Dyer, Colourist of the Depths and Master Dyer. Each character earns them separately. See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#achievements)
- Dye bottles now show their color, and single-clicking one shows the dye's name. Bottles you already have get their color back
- Dye bottles can no longer recolor clothing that a dye tub can't, such as reward robes, shrouds and hoods. Sandals and masks still take dungeon dye
- **This patch updates the client hue file.** Update through the launcher, or blends will look grey

See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#blends).

## ⛏️ Archaeology: Digging the War Ruins

- Digs now take 3 swings per stage, about half a minute in all, and leave an excavation on the ground that anyone passing can see. After each swing the target cursor comes back by itself
- Every finished dig turns something up. Most of it is war-layer spoil, about one dig in 5 gives a town's notable piece, about one in 28 a rare, and about one in 400 a legendary find that the whole shard hears about
- Single-clicking a find shows its tier and who unearthed it and when
- If your pack is full, the find goes to your bank box instead of the ground
- Decoded clues show their ruin and how promising the site is: looted, worked or undisturbed
- The five ruins are now equally likely, so Yew digs are no longer rare
- Dig sites no longer fall inside walls, houses or water. Old clues pointing at a bad spot move to a good one when you open them
- Use a tool within 5 tiles of the dig site and you're told which way to go
- New skill needs: Mining 70 for the shovel, Mining 80 for the pick hammer, Item Identification 70 for the brush. Below those, you're told what's needed and no tool use is spent
- Tools now spend a use per finished stage instead of per swing, so a bag of tools lasts about 200 digs
- If a half-dug clue changes hands, or its excavation fills back in, the dig starts again with the shovel
- New: the **Field Register**, 50,000 gp from Provisioners. Enter one of every find, page by ruin, and read the history of each. Common finds you enter count as spoil
- New **Collections / Archaeology** achievements, with the titles Ash Sifter, Antiquary of each town and War Historian

See [Archaeology](../custom-systems/archaeology.md).

## 🔭 Astronomy

- Dates in the Constellation Ledger, the constellation view and on star charts are now shown in US Central time
- A used discovery claim now comes back at midnight US Central time, 7 days after you hand it in, instead of at the exact hour you handed it in

See [Astronomy](../custom-systems/astronomy.md#the-rules-of-the-sky).
