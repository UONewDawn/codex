# SERVER PATCH 1.23

## November 18th, 2026

This patch brings Landfall, a Thanksgiving sea event, and the Settler's Ledger for new players. It also adds dungeon dye blends, a rework of archaeology digs, damage numbers you can customize, and a few changes to CTF.

❗**This patch comes with a required client update to 2.2. Older clients can no longer connect. Close the client, restart the launcher, and let it update.**❗

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Enhancements

- Several New Dawn gumps now use an easier-to-read font instead of the wide, blocky one
- House raffle and auction stones have updated gumps. Results are posted on Discord, and the winner (and the auction's seller) gets a Discord message
- Auction House listings with ordinary words like "spice rack" or "destard" are no longer refused
- Withdrawing gold from the bank now tells you how much was taken out. It used to show 0 or nothing at all
- The welcome message shows accounts older than a year in years and days, like "4y 139d"
- Order vs. Chaos: the underdog bonus still starts at 25% behind but now lasts until the gap closes to 20%, so it no longer flickers on and off. The war horse keeps its faction color after you dismount
- Pets set to follow now keep following when attacked, instead of turning on whoever hit them, and go back to following after a kill. Use `all guard` or `all kill` when you want them to fight
- Pets no longer attack your party members or their pets on their own. `all kill` on a party member still works
- Pets and other owned creatures that die in the dungeon of the week no longer count toward its kills or roll its bonus drops
- You can now cast Teleport inside Codex Rifts and the Holdfast. Recall, Mark and Gate Travel are still blocked
- On some nights an aurora lights the sky over the snowy north. You need the latest New Dawn client to see it, and you can turn it off in Options
- Astronomy dates are shown in US Central time, and a used discovery claim comes back at midnight Central, 7 days after you hand it in. See [Astronomy](../custom-systems/astronomy.md#the-rules-of-the-sky).

## 🦃 Thanksgiving: Landfall (November 20th – 30th)

Pilgrim ships from Delucia and Papua have broken through the fog, and the Lost Lands' natives have taken them as prizes. Get a **Lookout's Horn** from Elder Mercy Thornbury at the Landfall camp in Vesper once a day, and blow it on a boat in deep water, well away from the camp, to call a prize ship

- Three prize ships, each with its own jailer: Ssalith the Coil-Warden, Kruuth the Brood-Keeper and Chieftain Gorrum Redpaint. Every fight can be done solo and scales with each character aboard, up to 3
- Kill the jailer, free the pilgrims and sail them home to the camp pier. Everyone who helped gets 4 maple tally sticks, or 10 on their first landing of the day, and each pilgrim's story goes into the Elder's register with your name
- Between ships, return the pilgrims' lost belongings that drop all over Felucca, or take on camp work delivering letters for 2 tally sticks each, up to 3 a day
- Spend tally sticks on pilgrim clothing, dyes, ship banners and ship stains in four new Thanksgiving colors
- During the **Feast, November 26 to 29**, spend 10 minutes at the camp's table for a Landfall plate. The fuller the shard has laid the table, the better the plate
- New **PvM / Landfall** achievements and titles, and a collectible statuette for every crew member and jailer

See [Landfall](../custom-systems/landfall.md).

## 🌱 New Players: the Settler's Ledger

- New players get a **Settler's Ledger**, one per person. Each visit at least 18 hours after your last stamp inks the next one, up to 14, and missing a day never costs you anything
- On your first day, choose an order from the Ocllo quartermaster (Swordsman, Mage, Crafter or Ranger). It arrives at your bank with 2,000 gold and supplies
- Gifts at stamps 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 and 14 include the titles **Settler** and **Homesteader**, a cloak, a globe, a lantern and gold. Type `[ledger` to see your stamps
- The welcome screen for newcomers has been updated to show what the server has to offer
- Young characters now always start on Ocllo, whichever city you pick

See [Young Player Program](../custom-systems/young-program.md#the-settlers-ledger).

## 🎨 Dungeon Dyes

- Double-clicking a Tailor's Dye Kit opens a kit window with a **Craft** button for each dungeon dye. Pour in every dye material in your backpack with one click
- Shaded Dusty Rose (Terathan Keep) dye can now be crafted
- 24 new blends: pour one bottle each of two dungeon dyes together on the kit's **Blends** tab. After a blend, you wait 3 days before you can pour another, shared across all your accounts. Each recipe shows who poured it first
- New **Crafting / Dungeon Dyes** achievements, with the titles Dungeon Dyer, Colourist of the Depths and Master Dyer
- Dye bottles show their color, and single-clicking one shows the dye's name
- Dye bottles can no longer recolor clothing a dye tub can't, such as reward robes, shrouds and hoods. Sandals and masks still take dungeon dye

See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#blends).

## ⛏️ Archaeology: Digging the War Ruins

- A dig takes 3 swings per stage, about half a minute in all, and leaves an excavation anyone passing can see
- Every finished dig turns something up: mostly war-layer spoil, about one in 5 a town's notable piece, one in 28 a rare, and one in 400 a legendary find the whole shard hears about
- The five ruins are equally likely now, so Yew digs are no longer rare. Dig sites no longer fall inside walls, houses or water
- Within 5 tiles of the site, your tool tells you which way to go. If your pack is full, the find goes to your bank
- Same skill needs: Mining 70 for the shovel, Mining 80 for the pick hammer and Item Identification 70 for the brush. Tools use a charge per stage instead of per swing, so a bag of tools lasts about 200 digs
- New: the **Field Register**, 50,000 gp from Provisioners, to record one of every find, plus **Collections / Archaeology** achievements and titles

See [Archaeology](../custom-systems/archaeology.md).

## 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Adventures

- Every person in a crew of up to 4 now gets the full boss purse, from 5 Doubloons at Greenhorn up to 9 at Davy Jones. Crews bigger than 4 share 4 purses
- The purse and the bonus Doubloon chances go to players who dealt damage, and your pets' and summons' damage counts for you. They used to go to whoever got hit
- Doubloons are paid once per person. If you bring several of your own characters, the one that did the most damage is paid

See [Pirate Adventures](../custom-systems/pirate-adventures.md#doubloons).

## 🖥️ New Dawn Client 2.2

- Your target now gets a soft ring under its feet and a small arrow over its head, in its notoriety color, instead of the old bracket markers. The ring is also its health bar: green, then yellow, then red, and purple when poisoned. Turn it on or off with "Show target ring and arrow" in Options
- The Assistant's Next, Previous, Closest, Random and Set Last Target mark the target with the ring too
- A new **Clear Target Indicators** macro and Assistant hotkey hides the health bars the client keeps under your last targets. Double-clicking yourself no longer puts a bar under your own feet
- Options > Video > Zoom smoothing now lets you pick xBR Sharp, xBR Smooth or Sharp bilinear. xBR Smooth has an edge softness slider and can also smooth at normal zoom
- Walking on a moving boat is smooth instead of jerky
- The seabed and anything swimming under translucent water show through it again
- A ridden llama keeps its two-tone coat
- New Assistant hotkeys: **Stop Current Macro** and **Pause/Resume Current Macro**. Pressing a stop hotkey while recording now records it, so playback stops there
- The Assistant's buy agent always buys its whole list, since vendors take what you're short from your bank. The "Buy agents ignore player gold" option is gone
- Target by Type on a creature no longer mixes it up with an item that has the same number. A llama used to show as "stone wall"
- The Assistant window is fully solid at 100% opacity
- Razor is updated to 1.10.2.0, which fixes a crash when reading some gumps

## 🎯 Damage Numbers

- A new **Damage** tab in Options: pick the font, how long numbers stay up and how they move, and choose your own colors for damage you and your pets deal and take
- Optional extras, off until you turn them on: heals show as **+12**, misses as **Miss**, shield blocks as **12 blocked**, and spell, poison and bleed damage can each get a color. A combat log can write your numbers to the journal
- Show DPS moved to the Damage tab. You still only see numbers for your own fights and your pets'

See [Damage Numbers](../getting-started/damage-numbers.md).

## 🏴‍☠️ Capture the Flag

- Capture the Flag is always open now after a server reboot. Sign up at either book in the Dawn Event Center at any time
- A new queue window shows both teams, how many players each still needs, the last result and your War Tokens today. Pick a team or "Join either team", so friends can wait together
- A match starts once both teams reach the minimum and are within 1 player of each other. Stay in the Dawn Event Center while you're queued
- If you reconnect during a match, you go back to your team
- The daily War Token caps (150 from CTF, 150 from Conquest) are now per person across all your accounts
- Killing the same player again within 60 seconds, or a disconnected player, gives no points, and healing points are capped at 40 per match
- Only players who took part get the win bonus. A match tied on captures and points is a draw
- A teammate who logs out during a match no longer takes their captures or points away from your team. The tiebreaker used to count only the points of players still in the match

See [Capture the Flag](../custom-systems/capture-the-flag.md).
