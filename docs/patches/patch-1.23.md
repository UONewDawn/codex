# SERVER PATCH 1.23

## Release date TBD

## 🎨 Dungeon Dyes

- Double-clicking a Tailor's Dye Kit now opens a kit window: one row per dungeon dye with the material in the kit and a **Craft** button. Pour in every dye material in your backpack with one button, target a single stack, or drop a stack onto the kit
- Shaded Dusty Rose (Terathan Keep) dye can now be crafted
- 24 new blends: carry one bottle each of two dungeon dyes and pour them together on the kit's **Blends** tab to make one bottle of the blend. Each blend is dark-bodied with a glint of the second dye on the folds. Each recipe shows who poured it first on the shard
- After you pour a blend it steeps for 3 days before you can pour another, shared by every character on all of your accounts. Crafting regular dungeon dyes is not limited
- New **Crafting / Dungeon Dyes** achievements, with the titles Dungeon Dyer, Colourist of the Depths and Master Dyer. Each character earns them separately. See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#achievements)
- Dye bottles now show their color, and single-clicking one shows the dye's name. Bottles you already have get their color back
- Dye bottles can no longer recolor clothing that a dye tub can't, such as reward robes, shrouds and hoods. Sandals and masks still take dungeon dye
- **This patch updates the client hue file.** Update through the launcher, or blends will look grey

See [Dungeon Dyes](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md#blends).

## ⛏️ Archaeology: Digging the War Ruins

- Digs now take 3 swings per stage, about half a minute in all, and leave an excavation on the ground that anyone passing can see. After each swing the target cursor comes back by itself
- Every finished dig turns something up. Most of it is war-layer spoil, about one dig in 5 gives a town's notable piece, about one in 28 a rare, and about one in 400 a legendary find that the whole shard hears about
- Single-clicking a find shows its tier and who unearthed it and when
- If your pack is full, the find goes to your bank box instead of the ground
- Decoded clues show their ruin and how promising the site is: looted, worked or undisturbed
- The five ruins are now equally likely, so Yew digs are no longer rare
- Dig sites no longer fall inside walls, houses or water. Old clues pointing at a bad spot move to a good one when you open them
- Use a tool within 5 tiles of the dig site and you're told which way to go
- New skill needs: Mining 70 for the shovel, Mining 80 for the pick hammer, Item Identification 70 for the brush. Below those, you're told what's needed and no tool use is spent
- Tools now spend a use per finished stage instead of per swing, so a bag of tools lasts about 200 digs
- If a half-dug clue changes hands, or its excavation fills back in, the dig starts again with the shovel
- New: the **Field Register**, 50,000 gp from Provisioners. Enter one of every find, page by ruin, and read the history of each. Common finds you enter count as spoil
- New **Collections / Archaeology** achievements, with the titles Ash Sifter, Antiquary of each town and War Historian

See [Archaeology](../custom-systems/archaeology.md).

## 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Adventures

- The boss purse is now paid in full to every person in a crew of up to 4. It used to be 5 each for one or two players, then 10 split between everyone, so a crew of 3 got 3 each and a crew of 4 got 2 each
- Harder seas now pay more: the purse goes from 5 Doubloons at Greenhorn to 9 at Davy Jones. Crews bigger than 4 share 4 purses, so 5 people at Davy Jones get 7 each
- The map's difficulty window now shows the boss purse before you open the gate
- The purse goes to players who dealt damage. It used to go to whoever got hit, so a player who never took a hit could leave with nothing
- Your pets' and summons' damage now counts for you, both for the boss purse and for the bonus Doubloon chance on each pirate. Tamers used to miss out on those chances
- Doubloons are paid once per person. If you bring several of your own characters, the one that dealt the most damage is paid

See [Pirate Adventures](../custom-systems/pirate-adventures.md#doubloons).

## 🔭 Astronomy

- Dates in the Constellation Ledger, the constellation view and on star charts are now shown in US Central time
- A used discovery claim now comes back at midnight US Central time, 7 days after you hand it in, instead of at the exact hour you handed it in

See [Astronomy](../custom-systems/astronomy.md#the-rules-of-the-sky).

## 🦃 Thanksgiving: Landfall (November 20 – 30)

- A century after the Lost Lands were sealed, pilgrim ships from Delucia and Papua have broken through the fog, and the Lost Lands' natives have taken them as prizes. Pick up a **Lookout's Horn** from Elder Mercy Thornbury at the new Landfall camp on the Vesper shore (X, Y), once a day, or fish one up in deep water, at most one a day. Blow it on any boat in deep water at least 150 tiles from the camp and a prize ship comes out of the fog
- Three prize ships, each with its own crew and jailer: the **Ophidian** prize under Ssalith the Coil-Warden, the **Terathan** prize under Kruuth the Brood-Keeper, and the **Savage** prize under Chieftain Gorrum Redpaint. Every fight can be done solo, grows with each character aboard, and is for at most 3 characters
- Kill the jailer, strike the pilgrims' shackles, and sail them home to the camp pier. Everyone who helped gets 4 maple tally sticks, once per person per landing, and your **first landing each day pays 10**. Prize ships never pin your boat
- Each hold carries 4 to 6 pilgrims: families with their children, and people travelling alone. Everyone you bring home goes into the Elder's register with your name and the date. Open any entry to read the story of who they were in the Lost Lands and how they were taken
- Every 50 souls landed across the shard adds a course to the camp's Thanksgiving table, and the camp and the pilgrims' story grow as families arrive. Spend 10 minutes at the table during the **Feast, November 26 to 29**, for a Landfall plate in pewter, silver or gold, depending on how full the shard laid the table
- Spend maple tally sticks on pilgrim cloaks, hats and bonnets, dye tubs, runebook and dragon dyes, ship banners and ship stains, in four new Thanksgiving colors: sage harvest, cranberry glaze, roasted chestnut and autumn maple. The cloak is 110 tally sticks and a dye tub 120. See [Landfall](../custom-systems/landfall.md#maple-tally-sticks)
- **Between ships, no boat needed:** the pilgrims' lost belongings turn up on kills and catches all over Felucca. Return them to Elder Thornbury to add a line to that family's story. She also has **camp work**: carry a family's letter to the kin they came to find, in the town their story names. Camp work pays 2 maple tally sticks a piece, for up to 3 pieces a day. See [Landfall](../custom-systems/landfall.md#between-ships-camp-work)
- New **PvM / Landfall** achievements for each jailer, for pilgrims brought ashore, for camp work and for the Feast, with the titles Chainbreaker, Homebringer, Kinfinder and Feastgoer
- Every prize-ship crew member and jailer has its own **statuette** in the Thanksgiving colors, with a hidden collection achievement and title for each. Jailers drop theirs more often, and the statuette goes straight into the pack of someone who fought it

See [Landfall](../custom-systems/landfall.md).

## 🎯 Damage Numbers

- A new **Damage** tab in Options, just under Combat/Spells. Pick the font of your damage numbers (Classic, Bold, Small, Outlined or Large outlined), how long they stay up (half a second to 4 seconds), and whether they fade out, scatter sideways or merge hits that land together into one number
- Choose your own color for damage you take, damage your pets take, damage you deal and damage your pets deal. Everything starts out the way it always looked
- New, off until you turn them on: heals show as **+12** over whoever was healed, misses show as **Miss**, a hit partly stopped by a shield shows as **12 blocked**, and spell, poison and bleed damage can each get their own color
- Turn on the combat log to write each of your numbers to the journal's System tab, like "You hit a mongbat for 12."
- Show DPS moved from Combat/Spells to the new Damage tab
- You still only see numbers for your own fights and your pets'. Nobody sees anyone else's, party members included

See [Damage Numbers](../getting-started/damage-numbers.md).

## 🏴‍☠️ Capture the Flag

- Capture the Flag is now always open. Use either signup book in the Dawn Event Center at any time, no need to wait for staff to open it
- A new queue window shows both teams, how many more players each one needs, the match in progress and the last result, your War Tokens earned today, and anything that would stop you entering when the match starts (pets, a mount, being dead)
- Pick a team or choose "Join either team". A team can fill up to the arena minimum on its own, so friends can wait together. Past that, a team can't get more than 1 player ahead. A match starts once both teams are at the minimum and within 1 player of each other, and goes back to waiting if someone leaves during the last minute
- Stay in the Dawn Event Center while you're queued and when you accept the match. You can no longer join or accept from somewhere else
- You can only be queued for one battleground at a time
- If you reconnect during a match you go back to your team instead of being sent out of the arena
- The daily cap of 150 War Tokens from CTF, and the separate 150 from Conquest, is now per person, shared by all your accounts and characters
- Killing the same player again within 60 seconds, or killing a disconnected player, gives no points. Healing and cure points are capped at 40 per match
- Only players who took part in the match get the +5 win bonus and the win. A match tied on both captures and points is a draw with no winner
- Flag Thief and Flag Defender progress now counts only when the steal or return gives points
- A match that is cancelled before it begins (not enough players accepted) no longer counts as a win for anyone
- Fewer Discord posts and chat reminders while you wait: a reminder every 2 minutes instead of every 10 seconds

See [Capture the Flag](../custom-systems/capture-the-flag.md).
