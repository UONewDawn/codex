# Dueling

The dueling pits sit in the Dawn Event Center, beside the Capture the Flag sign-up. Fight a friend, settle a grudge with a rival, climb the monthly ladder or win the Saturday Cup.

Nothing is lost in the pits. Reagents, potions, bandages and arrows are free, your gear doesn't wear, and nobody dies: a killing blow knocks you out of the fight instead.

## Getting started

Type `[Duel` anywhere, or double-click the **Dueling Signup Board** in the Dawn Event Center. The board has five pages:

- **Challenges**: duels waiting for an opponent. Click one to accept it
- **Post a Duel**: set up your own challenge
- **Ladder**: this season's ranked standings
- **My Record**: your rating, your totals and your last 10 matches
- **Cup**: the next Saturday Cup, sign-ups and standings

The Dawn Event Center can be reached from any public moongate by picking the upper right option.

### Gorn, the Duel Announcer

Gorn stands among the pits and calls every fight: who steps into which pit, knockouts, rounds, sudden death, upsets and win streaks. He hosts the Saturday Cup too. Double-click him, or say **duel**, **ladder**, **cup** or **record** near him, to open the board.

## Posting and accepting a duel

You can post and accept duels from anywhere. Once a duel is accepted, everyone has **two minutes to reach Dawn**. The fight begins as soon as all of you are there and a pit of the right size is free.

When you post a duel, you choose:

| Option | Choices |
|--------|---------|
| Kind | **Casual** or **Ranked** |
| Format | 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 (casual only) |
| Time limit | 5, 10 or 15 minutes (casual only) |
| Rounds | Best of 1 or best of 3 (casual only) |
| Open to | Anyone, your guild, or one rival you pick |

- For 2v2 and 3v3, **your party is your team** and must be exactly the right size. Whoever accepts brings their own party of the same size
- **Ranked** duels always use the house rules: 1v1, 10 minutes, best of 1. Ranked needs an account linked to Discord
- A challenge stays up for 30 minutes, or until you log out
- When you post a challenge, players in Dawn and anyone who has dueled before get a message. Turn these alerts off with `[DuelAlerts`

After every duel, both sides are offered a **rematch** with the same rules. It starts if everyone accepts within 30 seconds.

## Who can duel

- Young players can't duel
- Criminals can't duel. Murderers can
- You can't join a duel within two minutes of fighting another player
- No pets or summons. Stable or release your followers first. Ethereal mounts are dismounted for you
- You can't duel while you're in a Capture the Flag or Conquest match, or signed up for one
- Two characters belonging to the same person can never be matched against each other, or put on the same team

## Rules in the pit

- You can only harm your opponents and only help your teammates. Nobody outside the pit can interfere, and you can't harm anyone outside it
- Teammates show green and opponents show orange
- Recall, Mark, Gate Travel, Teleport, Invisibility and summoning spells are blocked, and so are Hiding and Stealth
- No skill gains
- **Knockouts:** when you take a killing blow, you are knocked out and moved to the edge of the pit to watch. There is no corpse and nothing to loot, and it doesn't count as a kill for murder counts, fame, factions, guild wars or PvP achievements
- **Leaving:** walking out of the pit or disconnecting forfeits. In a team match, only you are out
- **Best of 3:** after each round, everyone is restored and the next round starts from the spawn points

### Sudden death

When the time limit runs out, **sudden death** begins:

- Healing and curing stop working
- Everyone is revealed
- Every 15 seconds, each fighter loses 5% of their maximum health

If nobody has fallen after 5 more minutes, the side with the larger share of its health left wins the round. An exact tie is a draw.

## Ranked ladder

Only ranked 1v1 duels count toward your rating.

- Everyone starts at **1200**. Your first 10 ranked duels move your rating faster while it settles
- Beating a higher-rated player is worth more than beating a lower-rated one
- **Repeat opponents count for less.** A second ranked duel against the same person within 24 hours counts for half, and a third for a quarter. After 3 ranked duels against the same person in a week, more duels against them don't count at all

### Monthly seasons

Each calendar month (Central Time) is a season. To place, play at least **3 ranked duels against at least 2 different people**.

When the month ends, the top three win:

| Place | Title | War Tokens | Trophy |
|-------|-------|-----------|--------|
| 1st | Season N Pit Champion | 1,000 | Gold |
| 2nd | Season N Pit Runner-Up | 600 | Silver |
| 3rd | Season N Pit Contender | 400 | Bronze |

Each person can place only once, however many characters they play. At the end of the season, every rating moves halfway back toward 1200, so everyone has a fresh run at the next one.

The ladder is posted to Discord every Monday, and the final standings when the season ends.

## War Tokens

Ranked duels pay War Tokens whether you win or lose:

- **Win:** 25 War Tokens
- **Loss:** 15 War Tokens
- **First ranked win of the day:** +25 War Tokens
- You can earn up to **500 War Tokens a week** from ranked duels. The week starts on Monday (Central Time)
- Repeat duels against the same person pay less, in the same way they count less for rating
- Casual duels don't pay War Tokens

Cup and season prizes don't count toward the weekly cap. Spend your tokens at the [War Tokens Store](../game-mechanics/currencies.md#war-tokens-store).

## The Saturday Cup

Every **Saturday at 8:00 PM Central**, the pits hold a cup.

- Sign-ups open 30 minutes before it starts. Sign up on the board's Cup page or with `[DuelCup join`, and leave with `[DuelCup leave`
- Be in Dawn when the cup starts, or you'll be dropped
- The cup needs at least 4 duelists
- One entry per person

The format alternates each week:

- **1v1 Cup**: ranked duels that count for the ladder
- **Shuffle Cup**: sign up alone and play on a new random team every round, mostly 2v2. Teams are balanced by rating and kept away from repeat teammates where possible. With 12 or more entrants it moves to 3v3

With 6 or fewer entrants, everyone plays everyone. With more, the cup plays up to 4 rounds, pairing players with similar scores who haven't met yet. A win scores 1. A draw, or sitting out a Shuffle round, scores half. A bye in a 1v1 cup counts as a win.

### Cup prizes

- **Finishing the cup:** 50 War Tokens
- **Top three:** another 300, 200 or 100 War Tokens, plus a gold, silver or bronze trophy
- **Winner:** the title **Reigning Cup Champion** until the next cup is won

If you leave the cup, disconnect, or miss a match, you're out and don't get a prize.
