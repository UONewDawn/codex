# Order vs. Chaos

Order vs. Chaos is the open-world war for Britannia. Fight for outposts across Felucca, earn War Tokens, climb the ranks, and help your faction win the monthly season.

## Joining the war

- Your faction is your guild's type. Join an Order or Chaos guild to take part.
- Changing sides is watched. After you switch between Order and Chaos, you earn no rewards and don't count toward captures for 3 days.

## Trophies

Kill enemy faction members to take their heads and ears. Turn them in at your faction's [Herald](#the-herald) for War Tokens and Faction Points.

| Trophy | War Tokens | Faction Points |
| ------ | ---------- | -------------- |
| Head   | 6          | 2              |
| Ear    | 3          | 1              |

- The top three enemy damagers share the trophies. Fight alone and you take the head and both ears
- Your pets' and summons' damage counts toward you
- Trophies decay after 2 hours
- You can turn in up to 3 trophies from the same enemy per day, counted across all of that player's characters

## Outposts

Outposts across Felucca can be captured by either faction, at any time. The client outlines each outpost on the ground and on the world map in the color of the faction that holds it, and marks the capture ground.

| Outpost       | What holding it grants your faction                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Watchtower    | [Rally](#rally): travel to it from your Herald                       |
| Field Camp    | A field medic who resurrects only your faction                       |
| Supply Depot  | +25 to your daily War Token limit, and 10% off at your faction store |
| Shrine of War | Counts double toward the season score                                |

### Watchtower

- SW of Britain, white tower
- North of Skara/Brit/Yew crossroads

### Field Camp

- Outside of Despise
- Old Trinsic Entrance

### Supply Depot

- Ocllo west coast building
- Southwest of Yew

### Shrine of War

- Old Chaos faction base

### How capturing works

Stand on the outpost's capture ground. Only living, visible players count, so hiding on the ground does nothing.

Taking an outpost from the enemy has three steps:

1. **Break the fortification.** Every owned outpost starts fully fortified, and attackers on the capture ground wear it down. Alone, this takes roughly 10 minutes, or longer when many faction members are online.
2. **Neutralize it.** Once the fortification is gone, hold the ground about 30 more seconds to tear down the enemy's banner. The outpost becomes neutral.
3. **Capture it.** Hold a neutral outpost for about a minute to claim it for your faction.

#### Numbers help, but not much

- Each extra attacker speeds things up a little less than the one before. Three attackers are faster than one, but not three times faster
- Every defender standing on the capture ground slows the attack hard. Two attackers against one defender barely move, and an even fight stops the attack completely
- To take an outpost the owners are defending, you have to drive them off the ground or kill them, not just outnumber them
- However many attackers show up, breaking the fortification always takes at least 6 minutes. That gives the owners time to answer the call
- The more Order and Chaos players are online, the longer a small group takes to break in

#### Defending

- While the owners hold the ground, or match the attackers one for one, the fortification slowly rebuilds. From empty, it takes about 10 minutes to fully rebuild
- **Every attacker you kill inside the outpost pushes the attack back.** Any progress toward neutralizing the outpost is lost, and 15% of the fortification comes back at once
- **You can break an assault.** If the attackers lose 3 more fights than they win inside the outpost within 15 minutes, their assault is broken. The outpost goes back to full fortification and can't be attacked for 15 minutes, and its defenders are rewarded. Trading kills evenly won't break an assault; the attackers have to be clearly losing
- The owning faction is warned in game when an assault starts, and again when the fortification falls to half and to a quarter
- Assaults move at half speed while none of the owning faction is online
- A freshly captured outpost can't be attacked for 30 minutes

#### The war banner

The outpost's war banner rises and falls with the fight, and ticks as it shifts. Single-click it to see who holds the outpost and how much fortification is left. The [War Map](#the-war-map) shows every outpost's fortification too.

### Rules of the ground

- Recall, gate and teleport can't take you into an outpost. Walk in, or [Rally](#rally). You can still recall out
- One character per account may be inside the outposts at a time, across all of them
- When an assault begins, the defending faction is alerted in game and on Discord

### Dying in an outpost

If the enemy faction kills you inside an outpost, **you keep everything**. Nothing is left on your corpse to loot, though your killers still take your head and ears.

- After a short wait as a ghost you return to life automatically, fully dressed and with your pets, at half health, mana and stamina
- You return **hidden**, at a random spot near one of the outpost's approaches, and the game prefers an approach with no enemies close by. Nobody can wait on the exact spot where you'll appear
- **Defenders return close by** while **attackers**, and everyone fighting over a neutral outpost, return farther out and have a longer run back
- The wait is 30 seconds. Die again within 15 minutes and it grows to 1 minute, then 2 minutes
- If a friend or a healer resurrects you before the wait ends, you get up right where you fell, with all your gear
- For 2 minutes after you return, you can't recall or gate out of the outpost, or Rally

This only applies when the enemy faction did most of the damage. If monsters, murderers, players outside the war or your own side kill you, or you die outside an outpost, it's a normal death and your corpse can be looted.

### Outpost rewards

| Action                                                              | War Tokens | Faction Points |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Capture an outpost                                                  | 15         | 5              |
| Neutralize an enemy outpost                                         | 8          | 2              |
| Defend an outpost under attack (per minute, up to 10 minutes a day) | 2          | 1              |
| Break an assault on your outpost                                    | 10         | 3              |

Captures and neutralizations only pay if the losing side had held the outpost for at least 20 minutes. Each player can be paid for capturing, neutralizing or breaking an assault on the same outpost once per hour each. Breaking an assault pays every defender who stood on the capture ground during it or is in the outpost when it breaks.

## Rally

Stand beside your Herald, open the [War Map](#the-war-map), and click Rally next to any Watchtower your faction holds.

- You can't Rally while dead, while criminal, or within 30 seconds of combat
- Your pets come with you

## War Tokens and Faction Points

- [War Tokens](../game-mechanics/currencies.md#war-tokens) are the Order vs. Chaos payout. You can earn up to 150 per day from the war, or 175 while your faction holds a Supply Depot
- Faction Points are not spent. They are your lifetime rank and never go down
- You can earn up to 50 Faction Points in any 24 hours

## Seasons

The war is fought in monthly seasons.

- Season score comes from outposts held (every minute counts, and a Shrine of War counts double), trophy points turned in, and [War Call](#war-calls) victories
- The winning faction earns +10% War Tokens for the whole next month, and its victory banner flies at its home
- **Underdog bonus:** a faction more than 25% behind in score gets +15% War Tokens, and breaks enemy fortifications 25% faster, until it closes the gap
- Weekly standings are posted every Monday
- The top three players on each side are named Champions of the Season

### What resets and what carries over

| Resets each season                                      | Carries over forever                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Season score                                            | Faction Points and rank                           |
| Your season points, kills, captures and defense minutes | Lifetime kills, captures, trophies and defense    |
| The season leaderboard                                  | Seasons played, seasons won, and your best finish |

## War Calls

Every so often the war picks a battlefield. A War Call names one outpost, and for 45 minutes it's the place to fight.

- **When:** a War Call can sound at any hour, once both factions have at least 2 active players online. There are at least 3 hours between War Calls, and no more than 3 in any 24 hours. You count as active when you're visible and have moved in the last 10 minutes or are standing in an outpost. Several characters on one account count once
- **Warning:** 10 minutes before it begins, both factions are told which outpost and where, and it's posted on Discord. Outposts held by the faction leading the season are picked more often, and the same outpost is never picked twice in a row
- **The fight:** for 45 minutes the outpost changes hands fast. A capture or a broken assault locks it for only 5 minutes instead of the usual 30 or 15
- **Winning:** the faction that holds the outpost for more of the 45 minutes wins **720 season score**, or 1,440 at a Shrine of War. Both sides have to show up: if nobody from the other faction takes part, the War Call scores nothing
- **Taking part:** spend 3 minutes on the capture ground, or help kill an enemy inside the outpost

| War Call reward                         | War Tokens | Faction Points |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Winning side, when both sides took part | 30         | 5              |
| Everyone else who took part             | 10         | 1              |

- Your daily War Token and Faction Point limits still apply
- If you have characters on both sides in the same War Call, you earn nothing from it
- Captures, kills and trophies inside the War Call outpost pay the same as anywhere else
- The [War Map](#the-war-map) marks the War Call outpost with a gold star and shows each side's time held, and `[OvcPoints` shows it too. The New Dawn client outlines it in gold

## The War Map

Type `[OvcMap` or ask your Herald. It shows:

- every outpost on the map in its owner's color, with a warning mark on any under attack and a gold star on the [War Call](#war-calls) outpost
- the War Call's time left and how long each side has held it
- each outpost's type, status, fortification and location
- the season score, days left, last season's victor and any underdog bonus
- your rank, season stats and War Tokens earned today

## The Herald

- Shows your rank, points to the next rank, and today's Faction Points and War Tokens
- Buttons for the Faction Store, War Map and Rally, plus a Buy option in its context menu
- Say **points**, **glory** or **score** for your standing, **trophies**, **head** or **ear** to turn in, or **buy** or **store** to shop
