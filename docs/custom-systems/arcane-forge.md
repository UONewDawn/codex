# Arcane Forge

Melt magic weapons and armor into **arcane dust**, and a blacksmith turns that dust into **slayer oil**. Rub the oil into any weapon and it hits like a slayer until the oil wears off.

## How It Works

1. **Melt.** A blacksmith melts magic weapons, armor and shields at an Arcane Forge into arcane dust.
2. **Hunt.** Monsters drop a reagent for their own slayer group. Undead drop grave marrow, dragons and serpents drop scale ichor, and so on.
3. **Craft.** A blacksmith combines dust, reagents, ingots and gold into a slayer oil.
4. **Coat.** Double-click the oil and target your weapon. It now counts as that slayer until its charges run out.

## Melting at the Arcane Forge

Arcane Forges stand in town smithies. To use one you need at least **90 Blacksmithy**.

Double-click the forge and target a magic weapon, armor piece or shield in your backpack. After a few seconds it dissolves into arcane dust.

- The item must be **identified** first
- Each melt costs **250 gold** to fuel the forge
- You can melt **50 items per day**. This is per account, and the day is the last 24 hours, not a calendar day
- The forge won't take slayer weapons, blessed or newbied items, or artifacts

### Dust Yield

Better magic gives more dust. Weapons count their damage or accuracy level, whichever is higher. Armor and shields count their protection level. Items that only have a durability bonus count as the lowest level.

| Weapon Level |   Armor Level   | Average Dust |
| :----------: | :-------------: | :----------: |
|     Ruin     |     Defense     |      1       |
|    Might     |    Guarding     |      1       |
|    Force     |    Hardening    |      2       |
|    Power     |  Fortification  |      3       |
| Vanquishing  | Invulnerability |      5       |

Every melt varies a little, so you may get somewhat more or less than the average.

**Alchemy** makes a real difference. With no Alchemy you get about 20% less than the table, and a Grandmaster Alchemist gets about 20% more. A GM smith who is also a GM alchemist averages 6 dust from a Vanquishing weapon. Melting also gives you a chance to gain Alchemy.

## Slayer Reagents

Every slayer group has its own reagent. Monsters in that group have a small chance to drop it when they die. It drops more often in a dungeon that is running the **Boon of the Slayer** bonus that week. See [Dungeon of the Week](dotw.md).

Champion spawn monsters don't drop reagents.

|    Reagent     |                                      Dropped By                                      |        Makes         |
| :------------: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------: | :------------------: |
|  Grave Marrow  |                 Undead: skeletons, zombies, liches, wraiths, mummies                 |  Silver Slayer Oil   |
| Brute's Blood  | Humanoids: orcs, ogres, trolls, ettins, cyclopes, ratmen, savages, pirates, brigands |  Repond Slayer Oil   |
|  Infernal Ash  |    Daemons and gargoyles: daemons, balrons, imps, succubi, ice fiends, gargoyles     | Exorcism Slayer Oil  |
| Elemental Core |     Elementals: air, earth, fire, water, blood, poison, ore elementals, efreets      | Elemental Slayer Oil |
|   Venom Sac    |     Arachnids: giant spiders, dread spiders, black widows, scorpions, terathans      | Arachnid Slayer Oil  |
|  Scale Ichor   |      Reptiles: dragons, drakes, wyverns, serpents, snakes, lizardmen, ophidians      | Reptilian Slayer Oil |
|    Fey Dew     |                 Fey: pixies, wisps, centaurs, unicorns, treefellows                  |    Fey Slayer Oil    |

## Crafting Slayer Oil

Slayer oils are under **Slayer Oils** in the blacksmith crafting menu. Like anything else you smith, you need to be near an anvil and a forge.

Every oil needs:

- **90 Blacksmithy** and **70 Alchemy**
- **20 arcane dust**
- **3 of the matching reagent**
- **10 ingots**
- **5,000 gold**

At 90 skill you will mostly fail. A Grandmaster succeeds about 95% of the time, and anything that puts you past 100 means you never fail. A failed attempt loses half the ingredients.

## Coating a Weapon

Double-click a slayer oil and target a weapon in your backpack or hands. The weapon now counts as that slayer.

- A coating holds **250** slayer hits, or **300** on an exceptional weapon
- A charge is only used when you hit a monster the oil slays. Hits on anything else are free, and so is travel, recalling or standing around
- Single-click the weapon to see what's left, for example **[silver oil: 212]**
- Coating with the same oil again refills it. A different oil replaces the old coating
- Weapons that already have a slayer can't be coated
- The coating stays on the weapon. If someone loots it, they get whatever charges were left

### The Slayer's Drawback

A coating carries the same drawback as a real slayer. While you wield it, monsters from the opposing group hit you for **double damage**. Know where you're hunting before you coat.

|    Oil    | Opposing Group (hits you harder) |
| :-------: | :------------------------------: |
|  Silver   |            Humanoids             |
|  Repond   |              Undead              |
| Exorcism  |        Elementals and fey        |
| Elemental |      Daemons and gargoyles       |
|    Fey    |      Daemons and gargoyles       |
| Arachnid  |             Reptiles             |
| Reptilian |            Arachnids             |

Slayer oil has no effect on other players.
