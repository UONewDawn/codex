# Coin Collecting

Britannia's money has a history, and some of it is still buried. [Treasure chests](../game-mechanics/treasure-hunting.md) can hold old coins, tokens and banknotes from three eras of Britannian history. Collect them all in a Coin Collector's Book.

## Finding pieces

- Any dug treasure chest can hold a piece. The higher the map level, the better your odds
- A chest holds at most one piece. When it does, you'll see "Something small glints among the treasure."
- Some issues turn up often; a few have been seen only a handful of times

### Where you dig matters

- Pieces tend to surface near the towns that issued them. Dig near Jhelom for Jhelom money
- Pirate money gathers around Buccaneer's Den
- Harbor coins turn up near the coast
- Royal Mint, Gargoyle and Orc pieces can turn up anywhere

### Varieties

Collectors know that many issues also exist in rarer varieties: specimen strikes, misprints, clipped coins and stranger things. The catalog doesn't list them; you'll know one when you find it.

Single-click a piece to see its era and, if it has one, its variety.

## The Coin Collector's Book

Bankers sell the Coin Collector's Book for **1,000 gold**.

- Drop a piece onto the book while the book is in your backpack to mount it. Mounting is permanent, and the piece can't be taken back out
- The book won't take a piece it already holds. A variety counts as a separate piece from the plain issue
- Double-click the book to browse the catalog: every issue, its history, and which pieces you hold
- The book shows "Varieties of this issue are known to exist" for issues with varieties, but never names a variety until you've mounted it

!!! tip
    The book is your collection, not your character's. Trade it, display it or guard it: the collection travels with the book.

## Achievements

| Achievement         | How to earn it                                  | Title              |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| The Crown Years     | Mount every issue of Volume I                   |                    |
| The Codex War       | Mount every issue of Volume II                  |                    |
| The Rebuilding      | Mount every issue of Volume III                 |                    |
| Numismatist         | Complete the Base Set: every issue in the catalog | Numismatist        |
| Master Numismatist  | Complete the Master Set: every issue and every known variety | Master Numismatist |

A variety also fills its issue's slot in the Base Set.

Achievements go to the player who mounts the piece that completes a volume or set. Buying a finished book doesn't earn them.
