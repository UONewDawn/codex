# Sea Encounters

Deep water is no longer a quiet place to fish. Fish from the deck of your boat in Felucca's deep water long enough, and something will come out of the fog to meet you.

## How it works

- Every cast from a boat's deck over Felucca's deep water raises your sea danger. A short stretch of fishing is safe; after that, each cast makes an encounter a little more likely
- The sea warns you first. Watch for messages like "A fog bank gathers along the horizon" or "You catch the creak of timbers that are not your own."
- When a ship comes, it sails up beside you, drops anchor, and throws a boarding line onto your deck. Double-click the line to swing across, and use the line on its deck to swing back. Your pets come with you, but you can't swing across while mounted
- Everyone aboard shares the encounter, and everyone's sea danger resets afterward
- Stepping away from fishing for about 15 minutes also lets your sea danger settle

## The ships

### Pirate Raiders

A ship flying the skull and bones pulls alongside, grapples your boat, and her crew swing across to board you.

- Their captain guards a trapped chest full of gold and treasure in the hold below
- You can't raise anchor until the captain is dead

### Distressed Fisherman

A battered fishing boat whose hold has been overrun by rats and something worse in the bilge.

- There's no deck crew, so you can head straight down into the hold
- Clear the hold, then talk to the fisherman. He gives everyone who helped a rare catch: a rare fishing item, or sometimes a new rare fish

### Derelict

A silent ship with no one at the helm and trapped crates below.

- Bring lockpicks: the crates are locked as well as trapped, and loose gold is scattered in the hold
- Something is waiting in the bilge water, so watch your step

### Ghost Ship

A spectral crew guards the deck, and their captain sits on a pirate's hoard below.

- Sometimes there's a pirate rare in the hold: a Ship in a Bottle, a Weathered Ship Wheel, a Pirate's Spyglass or a Barrel of Rum

Pirate Raiders are the most common sight. Ghost Ships are rare.

## Things to know

- You only reach a ship's hold after you've cleared her deck crew, including anyone who swung across onto your boat
- Encounter ships can't be steered or claimed
- Encounter ships don't stay forever. You get a warning before one weighs anchor, and anyone still aboard is put back on their own boat
- Once the encounter is won, the ship starts to sink. You have **two minutes** to get off, and anything left aboard or in her hold goes down with her

!!! warning
    Loot the hold before you leave. Once the last player is off a beaten ship, she sinks right away.

## 🎃 The Phantom Fleet

Creatures in the Halloween [rifts](rift-seasons.md) sometimes drop a **Phantom Fleet chart** marked with a spot at sea. It's super rare from rift creatures, and mini-bosses drop one far more often.

- Single-click the chart to see the X, Y it's marked at
- Sail to the mark. Then, standing on the deck of your own boat in deep water, double-click the chart to call one of four haunted ships
- Charts only work while the Phantom Fleet sails during the Halloween Rift Season. They don't expire, so you can save one for next year
- No running. While a Phantom ship's boss lives, your boat stays at anchor
- Each Phantom ship's hold also has a trapped treasure chest

### The fleet

| Ship                | Boss                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Dead Pirate Ship    | Captain Hollowmere the Drowned          |
| Zombie Sailor Ship  | Bosun Blackgut the Bloated              |
| Bone Ferry          | The Ferryman of the Last Crossing       |
| Pumpkin Head Ship   | Old Jack, the Headless Helmsman         |

- **Captain Hollowmere the Drowned.** He fades into the fog and steps out right beside you. Keeping your distance won't help.
- **Bosun Blackgut the Bloated.** He spews bubbling bilge around a player, and it bursts into poison. Move away fast, though there's little room in the hold.
- **The Ferryman of the Last Crossing.** He hooks whoever hangs back and drags them to his side. When he's badly hurt, he raises the drowned.
- **Old Jack, the Headless Helmsman.** He lobs lit jack-o'-lanterns that hiss for a moment and then burst into flame.

### Soul Coins

Each Phantom Fleet boss drops 8 to 12 [Soul Coins](../game-mechanics/currencies.md#soul-coins). Spend them with Mortlake the wreck warden at the Phantom Fleet Exchange on lanterns, grim decorations, and ship flags and stains in each haunted ship's colors.
