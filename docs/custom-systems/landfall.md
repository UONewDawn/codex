# 🦃 Thanksgiving: Landfall

A century after the Codex was destroyed and the way into the Lost Lands closed, the fog wall on the Lost Lands' sea has worn thin. Pilgrim ships from Delucia and Papua have sailed through it, looking for a way home to Britannia.

The Lost Lands' natives were waiting at the fog's edge. Ophidians, terathans and painted savages have taken the pilgrim ships as prizes and chained the families below decks. Free them, sail them to the Landfall camp at Vesper, and help lay the camp's Thanksgiving table.

Landfall runs from **November 20 to 30**. The Feast is **November 26 to 29**, Thanksgiving Thursday to Sunday. Days start at midnight US Central time.

## How it works

1. **Get a horn.** Elder Mercy Thornbury at the Landfall camp in Vesper gives you a **Lookout's Horn** once a day. You can also fish them up: fishing from a boat in deep water during the event sometimes brings up a waterlogged horn, at most one a day per person. Horns can be given to anyone: if you have no boat, give yours to a captain.
2. **Sail out and blow it.** Stand on the deck of any boat in Felucca's deep water, at least 150 tiles from the camp, and double-click the horn. A prize ship comes out of the fog, and you're told how many of you she's crewed to fight. The horn is used up only if a ship answers. She won't come within 150 tiles of the camp, so if you sail back toward Vesper before she reaches you, she sheers off and the horn is lost.
3. **Clear her deck.** She anchors alongside, two of her crew swing across onto your boat, and the rest guard her deck. Double-click the boarding line to cross.
4. **Break the hold.** Once the deck is clear, the hold opens. Four to six pilgrims are shackled below under their jailer: families with their children, and people travelling alone. Now and then a child has made the crossing alone.
5. **Free the pilgrims.** When the jailer falls, its key ring drops. Double-click each pilgrim to strike their shackles. They climb out and cross to your boat, so keep it beside the prize ship until everyone is free.
6. **Sail them home.** Take your boat to the Landfall camp pier at Vesper. When you arrive, the pilgrims step ashore, and everyone who helped and is still aboard gets **4 maple tally sticks** in their backpack, or in their bank box if the pack is full. Your **first landing each day** pays 6 more, so 10 in all. The bonus comes back 20 hours after you last had it, and the ledger shows when it's ready. Tally sticks aren't blessed, so you can lose them if you die before you bank or spend them. If everyone aboard was paid too recently, the pilgrims wait on your deck until someone can be thanked.

Fishers can also meet prize ships the ordinary way, by fishing deep water long enough. See [Sea Encounters](sea-encounters.md).

!!! tip
    Your boat is never pinned by a prize ship. If a fight goes badly, raise anchor and sail away. The ship drifts back into the fog.

## Who can take part

- A prize ship is a fight for **1 to 3 characters**. The horn won't sound with more than 3 characters aboard, and fishing never brings a prize ship to a deck that crowded.
- The fight grows with every character aboard: one more deck crew for each character beyond the first, and a tougher jailer. Every fight can be done solo.
- You don't need your own boat. A horn works on any boat you're standing on.
- Up to 3 people are thanked for each ship, one character each, starting with the ones aboard when she arrived and then anyone who fought aboard the prize. Tally sticks are paid once per person for each landing, however many of your characters are aboard. Characters down in your boat's hold count as aboard. After you're paid, you can't be paid for another landing for 25 minutes, and your horn won't sound until then.

## The prize ships

| Ship                                      | Deck crew                          | Jailer                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Ophidian prize** (Papuan pilgrims)      | Ophidian Corsairs and a Tidecaller | **Ssalith the Coil-Warden**   |
| **Terathan prize** (Delucian pilgrims)    | Terathan Grapplers and a Spitter   | **Kruuth the Brood-Keeper**   |
| **Savage prize** (families from all over) | Savage Reavers and a Drummer       | **Chieftain Gorrum Redpaint** |

### Deck crew

- **Ophidian Corsair:** hooks whoever keeps their distance and drags them close, with a dose of poison.
- **Ophidian Tidecaller:** brine pools under your feet. Move before it bursts.
- **Terathan Grappler:** a web line that holds you in place for a moment.
- **Terathan Spitter:** acid that keeps burning the planks it lands on.
- **Savage Reaver:** throws spears that leave you bleeding.
- **Savage Drummer:** while the drum beats, every reaver near it hits harder. Kill the drummer first.

### Jailers

- **Ssalith the Coil-Warden** wraps someone in her coils and squeezes. A friend or a pet can tear the coils loose by hurting her. Badly hurt, she sheds her skin and slips away across the hold.
- **Kruuth the Brood-Keeper** lays egg sacs that hatch into spiderlings if you don't break them in time. Near death she goes into a frenzy, with venom on every bite.
- **Chieftain Gorrum Redpaint** stops to daub on fresh blood paint, which heals him unless you hit him hard while he paints. Badly hurt, he calls up the spirits of his tribe.

The hold also has a treasure chest. Loot it before you leave: once every pilgrim is free, the ship starts to sink.

### Statuettes

Every prize-ship crew member and jailer has its own statuette, in the Thanksgiving colors: sage harvest for the ophidian crew, roasted chestnut for the terathans, autumn maple for the savages, and cranberry glaze for all three jailers. Jailers drop theirs more often than their crews do. The statuette goes straight into the pack of one of the people who fought it, more likely the more damage they did, and each one has its own hidden collection achievement and title.

## The Landfall camp

The camp is on the shore at Vesper, at **X, Y** (TODO: camp coordinates). Elder Thornbury stays after the fog closes, so you can still spend your tally sticks. Single-click Elder Thornbury and choose Talk, or double-click her, to open the **Landfall Ledger**:

- how many souls have come ashore, and how many courses are on the table
- when your next horn is ready, when your first-landing bonus is ready, when you can be thanked for another landing, and whether you have your Feast plate
- the day the fog closes and the last ships come
- the families and lone travellers most recently landed, and who brought them home
- the pilgrims' story so far, told in four chapters
- how much [camp work](#between-ships-camp-work) you've been thanked for today
- today's horn, camp work, and the camp's trade

### The register

Every family and lone traveller who comes ashore is written into the Elder's register, with the names of the people who brought them home and the day they landed. The register keeps every year's families for good. The ledger lists this year's latest arrivals: click the gem beside any name to read about them, or choose **The register** to see everyone ever brought ashore, newest first. The **Brought home by you** tab shows only the people you rescued.

Click an entry to read their story: who they were in Delucia or Papua, why they left, how they were taken at the fog's edge, what happened in the hold, the one thing they carried, and who they hope to find in Britannia. Every family's story is different.

Everyone who brought a family home and was still aboard at the pier is named in the register, even if they were thanked too recently to be paid again. Families who rowed themselves ashore are written in too.

### The camp

The camp grows as families land, and every 50 souls another course appears on the long table. Each landing, one of the settlers takes the name of someone you just brought ashore, so you can walk up from the pier and find them. The whole shard is told when the table reaches 6 courses and again when it's full. The story is told over the whole event: chapter 2 opens on November 22, chapter 3 on November 26 and the last, _The Last Ships_, when the Feast closes on November 30, the last day to bring ships home. If enough souls land across the shard, chapters 2 and 3 open a little sooner.

## Between ships: camp work

You don't need a boat for these, and they fill the time between prize ships. Both add a line to a family's register entry that stays there for good, and both pay from one shared allowance: **2 maple tally sticks** for each of your first **3 pieces a day** per person, so up to 6 (it resets 20 hours after your first one), however many characters you play. After that you can keep doing camp work for the story, just without the tally sticks. The ledger shows how many you've had today.

### Lost belongings

The captors stripped the pilgrims' bundles and threw what they didn't want overboard, and now it's turning up across Felucca. Any creature you kill, and any cast you fish, from a boat or the shore, can turn up something like _a sodden rag doll_. Single-click it to see the family name scratched into it.

Drag it onto **Elder Thornbury**. She tells you whose it was and who brought them home, and the family's register entry gets a line: _Returned: a sodden rag doll, found by you._ Belongings only belong to families already landed this year, so none turn up until the first ships come home. Each family gets one thing back. A belonging falls apart a day after it's found, so don't sit on it, and any left over crumble when Landfall ends.

### Letters to kin

Every family's story says who they hope to find in Britannia: an inn near Trinsic, the healers of Moonglow, a cousin in the New Minoc mines. Open the ledger and choose **Camp work**, and the Elder gives you a letter from a family landed this year, from one you brought home if there is one. Single-click the letter to see where it goes.

Double-click the letter and target any shopkeeper in that town. They read it and tell you what became of the kin, and the family's entry gets a closing line, for example: _Found the cousins who keep an inn at Trinsic. Letter carried by you._ You carry one letter at a time, and it lapses after two hours.

## The Feast

From **November 26 to 29**, the long table is set for the Feast. Stand or sit at the table and you're told when you start to count. Spend 10 minutes there and Elder Thornbury gives you a **Landfall plate**, one per person, placed in your bank box, and names you and the family you brought home to the whole table. The plate's metal depends on how full the table was when the Feast began, so everyone at the same Feast gets the same plate. Bring families home before the 26th:

| Courses laid | Plate  |
| ------------ | ------ |
| Fewer than 6 | Pewter |
| 6 to 11      | Silver |
| All 12       | Gold   |

While people sit at the table, the pilgrims say grace now and then. They thank someone at the table by name for the family they brought home, if anyone there brought one home this year.

Spend your tally sticks with Elder Thornbury. Most rewards come in four Thanksgiving colors: **sage harvest**, **cranberry glaze**, **roasted chestnut** and **autumn maple**.

A regular player who sails most nights earns enough over the season for one of the big rewards. Someone who sails for hours most days can take home two or three.

## Things to know

- All Lookout's Horns, lost belongings and letters crumble when Landfall ends.
- Lost belongings aren't blessed. Letters are.
- The **Landfall stone** at the camp keeps a count of the souls brought ashore each year.
- Pilgrims can't be hurt, and stand still on your deck until you reach the camp.
- If your boat is dry-docked or sinks, or you don't reach the camp within an hour, the pilgrims row themselves ashore in a coracle. They still count for the camp, but nobody is paid.
