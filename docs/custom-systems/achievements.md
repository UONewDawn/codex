# Achievement System

Track your accomplishments through New Dawn's comprehensive Achievement system. Complete challenges, reach milestones, and unlock exclusive titles.

## Overview

You can track your progress and leaderboard by typing `[achievements` in game.

To enable and see which titles you've earned, use `[titles`.

You can also check through the [game portal](https://portal.uonewdawn.com/achievements), where you'll find a full list of all achievements and see exactly which ones grant which title.

Achievements are tied to each individual character.
