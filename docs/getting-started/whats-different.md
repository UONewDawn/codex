# What's Different from Classic UO?

New Dawn builds on the classic Ultima Online foundation while introducing meaningful changes and custom systems. This guide helps veteran UO players quickly understand what makes New Dawn unique.

## Overview

- Single Felucca facet (full PvP)
- 700 skill points / 225 stat points
- Classic skills with some modifications

## Account

- Each player has three accounts.
- Only one account allowed per dungeon.
- Multi-client PvP is blocked.
- One house per account, 30 days decay timer, see [Housing](../game-mechanics/housing.md) page.
- Two boats per account, 14 days decay timer.

## Miscellaneous

- Horses can be tamed regardless of skill.
- All players start with one bond slot. It takes 24 hours to bond a pet. Bonded pets can recall with you. Tamers can acquire more slots with the [Animal Bonding Quest](../custom-systems/animal-bonding-quest.md).
- You can resurrect your dead pet at any stable. Single‑click the Veterinarian NPC and select "Resurrect Pet."
- There's no light filter, so the world looks (and feels) properly dark at night. Don't worry, you will get used to it in no time, you can buy Nightsight potions or use the Night Sight spell, they will last a long time.
- Dungeons have a 50% skill boost gain, except for crafting skills.
- When you set a skill pointing down it will decrease at the same time as you gain on a skill pointing up, you don't have to wait to be at skill cap to lower a skill.
- Recall charges inside a runebook don't require magery.
- You can't recall in or out of dungeons, instead, there are exit gates spread around.
- Spellbooks can only be crafted.
- Runebooks can be blessed using [Codex fragments](../custom-systems/codex-rifts.md#codex-fragments) and [Codex Bindings](../skills/crafting/alchemy.md#codex-binding).
- To avoid unattended gathering, all resource gathering activities will trigger the AFK captcha gump.
- Instead of Dex penalty when wearing armor, you instead lose stamina when taking melee damage, based on the armor you're wearing.
- All monsters and animals have a chance to drop their own statue, and an even rarer chance for that statue to be bronze.

## Custom Systems

For an in-depth overview of systems not listed on this page, visit the [Custom Systems](../custom-systems/index.md) page.

## Murder System

- Short-term kills under 5, will decay every 16 hours.
- Short-term kills equal or over 5, will decay every 24 hours.
- Long-term kills under 5, will decay every 24 hours.
- Long-term kills equal or over 5, won't decay anymore.

For more information visit the [Murder System](../game-mechanics/murder-system.md) page.

## Smuggler

You can find the Smuggler NPC in New Minoc at coordinates 2348, 200, under the stairs leading to the smuggling tunnel.

Another Smuggler can be found near the Vesper Inn at coordinates 2824, 980.

You can only buy from them during nighttime in‑game.

The Smuggler has unlimited restock, but their prices are slightly higher.

- 500 Reagents for 2.500 gold - 5 gold each instead of 3
- 500 Arrows for 3.000 gold - 6 gold each instead of 5
- 500 Bolts for 2.500 gold - 6 gold each instead of 5
- 250 Bandages for 1.750 gold - 7 gold each instead of 5
- 500 Blank Scrolls for 2.500 gold - 5 gold each instead of 3
- 50 Empty Bottles for 350 gold - 7 gold each instead of 5

## Spells

You can cast and keep spellbooks, runebooks, lanterns, candles and torches equipped.

In PvP the spell range has been reduced to 10 tiles, while Teleport is still 12.

[Mind blast](../game-mechanics/character/stats.md#mind-blast) damage is capped at 40.

When you attempt to cast poison on a mob, it will now consider your poison skill. At GM poisoning, you have a 50% chance to apply poison even if they're immune. It scales down from 50% (ie: at 55 poisoning, you have a 27.5% chance)

## Potions

Two second cooldowns on all potions, ten seconds on Heal.

Explosion potions, when used in Duels, CTF and Conquest, have an 8-second timer.

Explosion potions have a small random delay before they explode.

## Bulk order deeds

A minimum skill of 70.1 (real) is required to get a BOD.

You must have 85.1 (real) or higher for a chance to get a large BOD.

The BOD 6-hour time limit applies to your whole account.

## Skills

### Spirit Speak

Spirit Speak passively enhances your Magery summons, making them last longer and harder to dispel.

For more information visit the [Spirit Summoner](../custom-systems/spirit-summoner.md) page.

### Animal Lore

Animal Lore boosts your Magery summons, increasing their stats.

For more information visit the [Spirit Summoner](../custom-systems/spirit-summoner.md) page.

### Archery

500ms delay between shots when standing still.

The new Snare ability lets you immobilize a target for 3 seconds, but it can still attack or cast spells.

For more information visit the [Archery](../skills/combat/archery.md) page.

### Parrying

The new Shield Bash ability lets you absorb Fire Breath damage when active.

It can also be used in PvP, if active when you get hit by a melee attack, you will stun the opponent.

For more information visit the [Parrying](../skills/combat/parrying.md) page

### Resisting spell

In PvP, if you resist the spell, the base damage is reduced by 33% instead of 50%.

For more information visit the [Resisting Spells](../skills/combat/resisting-spells.md) page.

### Lumberjacking

New Heartwood Oil BOD reward, similar to Mining Prospector tool.

New Woodwarden Axe BOD reward, similar to Gargoyle pickaxe.

New Oak Reaper, Ash Reaper, Yew Reaper and Tree Giant monsters.

For more information visit the [Lumberjacking](../skills/resource-gathering/lumberjacking.md)

### Carpentry

Masonry lets you craft new items using stones.

For more information visit the [Carpentry](../skills/crafting/carpentry.md) page.

### Mining

You can now mine stones to use with Masonry.

For more information visit the [Mining](../skills/resource-gathering/mining.md)

### Cooking

Egg bombs can be used during combat to hide and have a 25 second cooldown.

New Cooking BODs system.

For more information visit the [Cooking](../skills/crafting/cooking.md)

### Alchemy

Explosion potions base damage against a player is between 5 min and 15 max. With GM Alchemy, it becomes 15 min and 20 max.
