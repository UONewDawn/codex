# Damage Numbers

The New Dawn client gives you a lot of control over the numbers that pop up over a fight. Open **Options** and pick the **Damage** tab. It sits just under Combat/Spells.

Out of the box nothing changes: damage you take is yellow, everything else is red, and the numbers look the way they always have. Everything below is up to you.

!!! info "Only your own numbers"
    You only ever see numbers for fights you're part of: hits you take, hits you deal, and hits your pets and summons deal or take. Party members and onlookers don't see your numbers, and you don't see theirs. That's the same as it's always been. The new options just tell you more about your own fight.

## Damage numbers

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Show damage numbers | Turns the numbers over a fight on or off |
| Show DPS | Adds your damage per second next to each number. It moved here from Combat/Spells |
| Font | Classic, Bold, Small, Outlined or Large outlined |
| How long numbers stay up | From half a second to 4 seconds. The default is 1.5 seconds |
| Fade numbers out | Numbers fade away instead of vanishing |
| Scatter numbers sideways | Numbers spread out a little left and right, so a fast fight is easier to read |
| Merge hits that land together | Hits on the same target within a fifth of a second add up into one number |

## More combat info

These are all off until you turn them on.

- **Show heals**: a heal shows as **+12** over whoever was healed. You see heals you give and heals you get, and the same for your pets. Bandages, heal potions, Heal and Greater Heal all count. Spirit Speak's channeling doesn't show a number
- **Show misses and shield blocks**: a swing that misses shows **Miss** over its target, for you and for whoever you're fighting. A hit that a shield blocked part of shows as **12 blocked**
- **Color spell, poison and bleed damage by type**: spell hits, poison ticks and bleeding each use their own color from the list below, so you can tell at a glance what's hurting. Weapon hits keep the who-hit-whom colors. A spell bounced back by Magic Reflection counts as spell damage

## Colors

Click a color box to choose a new one.

| Color | Used for |
|---|---|
| Damage you take | Yellow to start |
| Damage your pets take | |
| Damage you deal | |
| Damage your pets deal | |
| Spell damage | Only with "Color spell, poison and bleed damage by type" on |
| Poison and bleed | Only with "Color spell, poison and bleed damage by type" on |
| Heals | Green to start |
| Misses and blocks | Gray to start |

The four damage colors start out red, which is how damage has always looked.

## Combat log

Turn on **Write my combat numbers to the journal** and every number also goes into the System tab of your journal, in the same color as the number:

- "You hit a mongbat for 12."
- "A giant spider hit you for 8 (poison)."
- "Your pet Rex hit a mongbat for 5."
- "You healed Bob for 10."
- "You missed a mongbat."

The log keeps writing your damage even with damage numbers turned off. Heals and misses go into it only when those are turned on.

!!! note "Needs the latest client"
    Heals, misses, blocks, damage colors by type and the pet colors all need the latest New Dawn client.
