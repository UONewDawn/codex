# Client

The New Dawn client is ClassicUO, built for this shard. On top of the client you know, it has a built-in assistant and lets you change selected art and UI graphics.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-robot-outline:{ .lg .middle } __Built-in Assistant__

    ---

    Razor, rebuilt inside the client. Same tabs, same options, and it runs on every platform.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Built-in Assistant](../built-in-assistant.md)

-   :material-palette-outline:{ .lg .middle } __Custom Art & Gumps__

    ---

    Replace item art and UI graphics with your own images. Covers where files go, how to name them, and what can be replaced.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Custom Art & Gumps](../client-customizations.md)

-   :material-package-variant-closed:{ .lg .middle } __Custom Packs__

    ---

    Don't want to make your own? Download ready-made packs of reagents, bags, spell icons and more.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Custom Packs](../custom-packs.md)

</div>
