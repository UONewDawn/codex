# Parties

Group up with friends to hunt, share fame and karma, and look after each other's corpses.

## Party size and invites

- Parties can hold up to **40 members**
- The leader can always invite. The leader can also give individual members permission to invite new players. Pending invites count toward the 40
- The party window shows the names of members who are out of range

## Passing the lead

The leader can make anyone else the leader with the **Lead** button on that member's row in the party window. Everyone is told who leads now. The old leader keeps permission to invite.

If the leader logs out, the party carries on. The lead goes to the longest-standing member the leader allowed to invite. If nobody can invite, it goes to the longest-standing member. A party of two still ends when either of you logs out.

The leader's **Disband** button still breaks up the whole party.

## Merging parties

Two parties can join up without re-inviting everyone.

1. The leader clicks **Merge** in the party window and targets anyone in the other party
2. The other party's leader gets 30 seconds to accept
3. Everyone in their party moves into yours at once. You stay the leader

The merged party can't go over 40 members, counting your pending invites. Invites the other party had pending are cancelled. Members from the other party start without permission to invite, so give it back to anyone who should have it.

## Sharing fame and karma

Fame and karma sharing is on by default. The party leader can turn it on or off with the **Share** button in the party window, or with `/share`.

When sharing is on, the fame and karma from a kill are split evenly across the party. Only members within 40 tiles get a share.

## Looting your corpse

Your party can loot your corpse by default. Each member chooses this for themselves. Turn it off with the **Loot** button in the party window, or with `/loot`, if you want to keep your corpse to yourself.

The Loot button always shows whether your party can actually loot your corpse.

!!! warning
    If you log out, you leave your party, and looting your corpse becomes a criminal act again.

## Fighting within a party

- Attacking a party member or their pet doesn't flag you as a criminal
- Party members still appear blue to each other, and you stay blue to everyone else
- Killing a party member doesn't give a murder count

## Commands

Type these in party chat.

| Command  | Description                                              |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/add`   | Invite a player (leader, or members with permission)     |
| `/share` | Turns fame and karma sharing on or off (leader only)     |
| `/loot`  | Turns whether your party can loot your corpse on or off  |
| `/quit`  | Leave the party                                          |
