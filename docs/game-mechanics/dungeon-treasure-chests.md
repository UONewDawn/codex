# Dungeon Treasure Chests

Locked, trapped chests spawn throughout Britannia's dungeons. Pick the lock with [Lockpicking](../skills/stealth-and-thievery/lockpicking.md), disarm the trap with [Remove Trap](../skills/utility-and-support/remove-trap.md), and claim gold, gems, scrolls, magic gear and, if you're lucky, the dungeon's own dye material.

## Chest levels

| Level | Lockpicking needed | Trap      | Gold       | Gems              |
| ----- | ------------------ | --------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| 1     | 57                 | Dart      | 30 - 129   | Sometimes, small  |
| 2     | 72                 | Explosion | 70 - 169   | Sometimes         |
| 3     | 84                 | Poison    | 400 - 700  | Always, 1-2 stacks |
| 4     | 92                 | Explosion | 700 - 1200 | Always, 1-3 large stacks |

If your skill is too low, you'll see "You don't see how that lock can be manipulated."

Higher-level chests also hold reagents, scrolls, potions, wands, and magic weapons and armor. Level 4 chests carry the strongest magic equipment.

!!! tip "Telling chests apart"
    Level 1 chests are always crates. Barrels only appear at level 2, and kegs at level 2 or 4. A dart trap means level 1, a poison trap means level 3.

The trap goes off when you open the chest after unlocking it, not while you pick it.

## Respawns

- Once a chest is unlocked, by Lockpicking or magic, it breaks apart after **5 minutes** so a fresh chest can spawn in its place
- Anything left inside spills onto the ground, where it decays like any other dropped item

The Unlock spell only works on level 1 and level 2 chests.

## Dungeon dye material

Dungeon treasure chests (level 2 and up) have a chance to contain the [dye material](../custom-systems/dungeon-dyes.md) of the dungeon they're found in.

- Higher-level chests have a better chance and contain larger stacks
- Chests in the current [Dungeon of the Week](../custom-systems/dotw.md) get a second chance at dye material
- Chests disarmed with Remove Trap before being picked have better odds
- Dye material only appears when the lock is picked with Lockpicking. Magically unlocked chests won't contain it

## Masterwork chests

Some level 4 chests spawn with a masterwork lock. Single-click a chest and look for **[masterwork lock]**.

- Masterwork locks need **95.0 Lockpicking** to attempt
- They hold far more gold and extra magic equipment
- They have the best odds at larger stacks of dungeon dye material, and the best odds at a hidden compartment

## Hidden compartments

Grandmaster lockpickers (100.0) have a small chance to find a hidden compartment when picking a level 4 dungeon chest. Masterwork chests have the best odds.

The find goes straight into your backpack. Hidden compartments hold rare items found nowhere else, including:

- A coffer for each dungeon, in that dungeon's own color
- A sturdy quicksilver lockpick that rarely breaks
- A gunmetal lantern
- And stranger things still

| Dungeon       | Coffer                     |
| ------------- | -------------------------- |
| Covetous      | Aurum gold coffer          |
| Deceit        | Phantom gray coffer        |
| Despise       | Envy green coffer          |
| Destard       | Dragon's scale red coffer  |
| Fire          | Inferno orange coffer      |
| Hythloth      | Abyssal black coffer       |
| Ice           | Glacial blue coffer        |
| Shame         | Forlorn violet coffer      |
| Terathan Keep | Dusty rose coffer          |
| Wrong         | Judgment blue coffer       |

Your first find earns the **Safecracker** achievement and title.

## Traps

Disarming a chest's trap with Remove Trap lets you salvage part of the mechanism:

| Chest              | Salvage                    |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Level 1            | Crossbow bolts             |
| Level 2            | Lesser explosion potion    |
| Level 3            | Poison potion              |
| Level 4            | Explosion potion           |
| Masterwork level 4 | Greater explosion potion   |

- Chests disarmed before being picked (level 2 and up) hold an extra stack of gems and have better odds at dungeon dye material
- A trap disarmed by a Grandmaster (100.0 Remove Trap) doubles the chance of finding a hidden compartment when that chest is picked
- The Magic Untrap spell doesn't work on dungeon chest traps, so it gives none of these bonuses

!!! tip "Bring a partner"
    Disarm first, then pick. A trapper and a lockpicker make a good team.

### Level 4 traps

- Explosion traps on level 4 chests deal at most 96 fire damage, so they can no longer kill most characters outright. Anyone within 3 tiles is caught in the blast
- A GM Remove Trap can always attempt to disarm a level 4 chest trap, though the strongest traps can still fail
